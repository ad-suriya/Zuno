# Zuno

## One-line description

Zuno is a voice-first, native-language financial opportunity
verification assistant that helps Indian users investigate
suspicious financial offers before putting money at risk.

## Problem

People receive financial opportunities through:
- WhatsApp
- Telegram
- phone calls
- social media
- email
- friends/relatives
- in-person approaches

They may not know:
- what claims matter
- what should be verified
- which warning signals matter
- where to verify information

## Solution

Zuno:
1. Understands the user's story.
2. Collects available evidence.
3. Extracts claims and entities.
4. Asks adaptive questions.
5. Checks trusted sources.
6. Identifies warning and reassuring signals.
7. Explains the evidence.
8. Provides safe next steps.

## What Zuno is NOT

Zuno is not:
- a trading platform
- a stock predictor
- an investment advisor
- a broker
- a portfolio optimizer
- a financial product marketplace
- a generic chatbot
